# NOVELTY AUDIT 2 — the v1.1.0 claims (C1–C5)

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Scope:** the five claims added in
v1.1.0, audited under the campaign's claim gate (exact category,
independent second path, targeted literature search). This note
distils the gate record; the full gate, including the second
paths' code and result files, is in the campaign record
(Wave 28). Verdict categories as in `data/novelty-audit.md`:
**(i)** explicitly published; **(ii)** known-equivalent /
implicit; **(iii)** not found in the sources searched — category
(iii) is **not** proof of novelty and is never worded as such.
Every number in C1–C5 survived an independent second path with
fresh code; the audit below concerns only the literature
position and the approved wording.

## C1 — Cartography: unit terms are naive (`data/cartography.md`)

- Census statement (41/41 unit terms naive; 46/46 wrong entries
  on the tensor's support; 8 orbits / 21 of 729 positions):
  **(iii) not found in the sources searched.**
- Nearest neighbours, disclosed: Heule–Kauers–Seidl
  (arXiv:1905.10192) classify the monomials inside one product's
  expansion by "type" — type-3 monomials are the naive products
  and must survive. That is bookkeeping of the Brent equations,
  **(ii)**-level background vocabulary, not a census of a
  decomposition's unit terms. Blankline Research (Jan 2026, not
  peer-reviewed) names four "anchor" products in *Laderman's
  scheme only* — a partial prior observation of the Laderman
  case; it covers no other scheme, no drop states, no orbits,
  no statistics.
- Gap: the Heule–Kauers–Seidl database (17,000+ schemes) was
  not censused; the claim is scoped to the campaign bank.
