# One-signedness: 11,777 unit-residual states, all +E_e; unit terms 2,010 positive vs 1 negative

**Date:** 2026-11-24. Two exact censuses over named finite
pools held by the campaign. A *unit-residual* (wrong=1) state
is a term-sum differing from the multiplication tensor T in
exactly one entry; its residual is ±E_e for the affected
entry e — +E_e when the state is missing one unit at a
support entry (have 0 / want 1), −E_e when it carries an
extra unit (have 1 / want 0, or a negative unit term's drop).

## The claim, at its approved wording

"Across every unit-residual near-miss state this campaign has banked, the residual is one-signed without exception: 11,777 on-support wrong=1 states (1,282 of the 22-term class and 10,495 of the 25-term class, generated by unit-term drops from verified schemes and their shear variants) have residual +E_e — a single missing entry on the tensor's support — in 11,777 of 11,777 cases. The matching sign census of unit terms themselves finds the same asymmetry: in our 53-file scheme bank and in the complete Heule–Kauers–Seidl population of 17,376 rank-23 schemes, unit terms number 2,010 positive against exactly 1 negative — and the single negative unit term is proj-24's off-support term, whose drop state is the campaign's only −E_e state, at an off-support entry, with 23 terms. No on-support −E_e state is known to us in any of this material. This is a census of the named pools, not a theorem: a rank-22 scheme, if one exists, need not arise from these pools, and the pattern's force is that a whole class of repair constructions (opposite-sign state surgery) is starved by it — the manufacturing of an on-support −E_e state is the named precondition for reviving that class."

The HKS positive tally (1,882) reproduces the v1.3.0 census
of `data/hks-census.md` exactly, which validates the sign
scan end-to-end against an independently published count in
this repository.

## Why it matters, at the strength stated

Opposite-sign surgery — combining two wrong=1 states whose
residuals cancel — needs a −E_e state on a support entry.
The censuses say the named pools contain none, and exactly
one negative unit term anywhere in them. Manufacturing an
on-support −E_e state is therefore the named precondition
for reviving that construction class. The censuses are
measurements of these pools, not a theorem: a rank-22
scheme, if one exists, need not arise from these pools.
