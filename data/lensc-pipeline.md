# The lensC pipeline: 612 shear variants, 10,495 wrong=1 states, 167,920 repair cases staged

**Date:** 2026-11-24. A pipeline census over ℚ on one named
parent: lensC, the campaign's own exact rank-26 block
construction (the rank-26 point of `data/ladder.md`). Before
anything was counted on it, the parent was re-judged fresh:
`verify.py` VERIFIED, exact rank 26, 0/729 wrong (the
campaign's standing C0 gate — a parent is judged before any
family is derived from it).

## The claim, at its approved wording

"Our own rank-26 scheme lensC carries 51 identical-factor pairs, the most of any scheme in our bank. Its full shear family — 612 variants, every one exact-verified and new — yields 10,495 distinct 25-term wrong=1 states by unit-term drops, all of the standard flavour (these are the 25-term half of the Item 10 census). From them we derived 10,495 free-4 repair instances (167,920 cases). The scope matters: a repair of one of these states would be an exact rank-**25** scheme — the first rung of the ladder down from 26 — not a rank-22 candidate. A 1,792-case selection has been built into a solver kernel; the family is staged, not decided."

## Pipeline numbers

- **Pairs.** lensC carries 51 identical-factor pairs — the
  most of any scheme in the campaign bank (cn122 has 7;
  Laderman none).
- **Shear family.** Each pair generates shear variants
  (exact rational shears along the shared factor): 51 pairs ×
  12 settings = 612 variants, every one exact-verified,
  distinct, and new to the bank; 408 of 612 have entries
  outside {0, ±1}.
- **Drop states.** Unit-term drops across the parent and its
  variants give 10,495 distinct 25-term wrong=1 states, all
  of the standard flavour (on-support, residual +E_e) — the
  25-term half of the census in `data/one-signedness.md`.
- **Instances.** From the 10,495 states, 10,495 free-4 repair
  instances (167,920 covering cases) were derived with the
  generalised driver. A 1,792-case selection (the 19 parent
  instances plus a deduplicated, ranked variant selection)
  has been built into a solver kernel; the family is staged,
  not decided.

**Scope, repeated because it is the easy one to lose:** a
repair of a 25-term state assembles an exact rank-25 scheme —
the first rung of the ladder down from 26. These instances
are not rank-22 searches, and no number in this record bears
on rank 22 directly.
